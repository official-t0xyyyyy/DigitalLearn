ABOUT THE PROJECT

Digital confidence starts with the right guidance.

Digital Saathi is an educational frontend project designed to help people understand common Indian digital identity and document services. It breaks down processes into approachable learning modules and points learners toward official resources.

This is an independent training portal, not a government website. Service rules and steps can change, so always confirm details on the official portal.

MODULE 01 / DIGITAL DOCUMENTS

Your documents, available digitally.

DigiLocker is a platform for accessing and storing digital documents issued by participating organisations. Issued documents can be accessed through your account.

  1. 01
    Open the official portal

    Visit DigiLocker using the official link in Resources. Check the web address before signing in.

  2. 02
    Sign in or create an account

    Follow the current instructions on the official service. Keep credentials private and never share an OTP.

  3. 03
    Find issued documents

    Use the service's document search or issued-document area to locate records available to you.

  4. 04
    Access and verify

    Review the document details and follow the portal's guidance for viewing or sharing it.

ⓘ

Remember: This training page does not sign you in or retrieve documents. Complete any real action only on the official portal.

NEED A LITTLE CLARITY?

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to common questions about this learning portal and digital services.

Visit official resources →
Is this an official government website?

No. Digital Saathi is an educational college-project frontend. It is not affiliated with the Government of India or any government department.

Does this website collect Aadhaar or PAN details?

No. This website has no forms for Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, OTPs or passwords. Do not enter sensitive identity details into unofficial websites.

Can I download my documents from this portal?

No. The portal provides learning guidance and links to official services. Open the relevant official website to access your own account and documents.

Are the service steps always the same?

Not necessarily. Government portals may change their processes, eligibility rules or fees. Check the latest instructions on the official service before taking action.

What should I do if someone asks me for an OTP?

Never share an OTP, password or PIN with another person. Enter an OTP only when you initiated the action on a verified official service and understand what it authorises.

Will my learning progress be saved?

Module completion and quiz progress are stored locally in your browser. They are not sent to a server. Clearing browser data may remove this progress.
